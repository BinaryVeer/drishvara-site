# Source and timing review — 21 November 2026

This continuation implements seven source-bound transit rules and three conditional review protocols in the private English/Hindi QA view. It preserves the 38 earlier factor rules, public runtime, location approvals, Word publication system and database gates.

## Source resolution

Phaladīpikā, V. Subrahmanya Sastri, second edition (1950), chapter 26, was checked against scanned printed pages 286–289 (carrier image numbers 321–324). The source register records image URLs, SHA256 digests of retrieved image bytes, printed/carrier pagination and visual-check status. The scans are research references, not rehosted book pages.

Mercury's eleventh-house transit pairs with twelfth-house vedha, with the Moon exempt (printed p.288). A digital translation giving eleventh-house vedha is rejected for this edition. All 36 pairings and Sun/Saturn and Moon/Mercury exceptions were checked against these scans. Seven visible receivers are evaluated; Rahu/Ketu receiver rules are excluded. Node-only obstruction is explicitly sent to lineage review. No universal lineage or complete scholarly approval is claimed.

Moon-relative sign geometry is computed from snapshot longitudes, not trusted caller-supplied house numbers. Nine unique grahas, matching longitudes/signs, a stable audited natal Moon and a qualifying margin are required. A sign-boundary case withholds the transit review. A position outside the source list does not become an adverse prediction. A listed position without vedha does not become a whole-day favourable verdict. Chapter 26's additional strength/aspect qualifications remain relevant.

## Daśā-inception and career review

BPHS 48.8 requires birth and inception conditions. The evaluator recomputes the existing assumed-margin Daśā envelope from the audited birth data rather than accepting an unrelated caller envelope. A reference instant within an uncertain period boundary withholds period factors.

For a qualified major period after the birth-clipped first period, five inception-range samples are calculated at the birth place, explicitly as a diagnostic convention. These are sparse samples under assumed numerical margins, not a proof of continuous sign stability, a relocation rule or independently verified inception houses. The birth-clipped first period is never presented as true inception. Unsupported ephemeris years are withheld. Sampled planetary sign agreement is not full Shadbala, auspicious-house synthesis or a favourable/adverse result.

BPHS 48.9's tenth-lord association with fifth/ninth lords is tested separately from whether the tenth lord is the major-period lord. Subperiod presence is not substituted for the verse's major-period condition. No promotion, job or benefit claim is assigned.

## Marriage-source applicability and factors

Praśna Mārga 20.67 explicitly refers to birth-ascendant planetary review. Its 20.65–66 factors were compared between the digital Sanskrit carrier and a B. V. Raman Part II English translation carrier. This is a bounded text comparison, not independent authentication of the hosted print recension.

The supported subset identifies seventh-house occupants, seven-visible-graha sign aspects, seventh lord, that lord's natal sign lord and stable D9 sign lord, Venus and Moon. It checks whether stable major/subperiod lords match that set and whether Jupiter is in the supported first/fifth/ninth signs from the seventh lord's natal or D9 sign. All are partial source-factor matches, not marriage windows.

The Lagna versus Lagna-lord Navāṁśa clause has an English-wording difference and is omitted. The optional Rahu reading and remaining transit clauses are not silently merged. D9 uncertainty, natal Lagna uncertainty or period-boundary uncertainty withholds the associated review. No question-chart agreement, partner-character judgement, event probability or marriage date is inferred.
