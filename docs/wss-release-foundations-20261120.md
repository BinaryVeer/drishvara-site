# What Stars Say — release foundations, 20 November 2026

## Scope and actual release state

Baseline `9a6f2992afd82fd7ae3d8879647e8a48548fb439` was confirmed on main,
with a successful Vercel deployment. This increment addresses the remaining
timezone reproducibility, independent numerical arithmetic, chart presentation
and candidate storage-policy checks. It does not approve full personal forecasts
or turn on subscriber storage. A working research chart is not a completed
public prediction service.

## Implemented

- Frozen IANA **2026f** offsets replace browser Intl/ICU in the WSS birth and
  reference-date resolver. All **221** timezone identifiers used by **3,266**
  approved places are covered, plus three compatibility identifiers. The wheel
  is pinned as `tzdata==2026.6`, verified against its publisher's SHA-256, and
  regenerated byte for byte in CI. Apache/public-domain notices are retained.
- **83,692** Python ZoneInfo comparison probes cover every transition boundary
  and interval midpoint in the generated tables. Another **16,330** checks
  resolve five representative dates for every approved catalogue entry.
  Two high-latitude entries retain their explicit Lagna restriction.
- The supported local-date envelope remains 1900–2100. The offset tables have
  padding on either side to resolve local/UTC boundary cases. Historical IANA
  representative-zone records do not certify every locality before 1970;
  future offsets follow frozen rules and may need a reviewed update.
- Every report and precision audit carries the timezone version and wheel
  checksum. Cross-version audits are rejected. Astronomy Engine 2.1.19 remains
  the pinned MIT production candidate; its built-in Delta-T and UTC-as-UT1
  approximation are disclosed. No Swiss runtime code is shipped.
- Separate Python Decimal Varga/Daśā arithmetic references are generated from
  the 160 synthetic Swiss/Moshier cases. They do not import the JS formula being
  tested. Both named year conventions are tested; this is arithmetic validation,
  not approval of a universal traditional year convention or event prediction.
- Daśā date ranges use explicit lunar-angle and birth-clock assumptions. They
  withhold near a Nakshatra boundary and never claim statistical confidence.
  The bilingual preview labels the ranges as assumptions, while exact personal
  event dates remain withheld. Unqualified active-period claims are removed
  from nested source evidence and month/year summary outputs.
- Audited D1/D9/D10 rows now have accessible **North and South Indian SVG
  diagrams**, with layout switches and the original complete house details.
  The graphics do not infer or calculate additional placements.
- The encrypted profile format preserves the selected earlier/later DST
  occurrence and timezone version. Legacy encrypted payloads still decrypt,
  but every restored profile requires governed place/timezone revalidation.
- Candidate Auth/RLS denies missing anonymous-user claims instead of assuming
  a permanent account. **16 real PostgreSQL policy checks** pass in disposable
  PGlite, covering owner/other user, anonymous/missing claims, forbidden UPDATE
  and owner deletion. JWT helpers are simulated: these checks do **not** replace
  hosted Supabase Auth integration or an independent security audit.

## Source review

The existing five source IDs were rechecked against their cited text editions.
The textual leads are recorded in
`data/quality/jyotisha/wss-source-review-20261120.json`. BPHS period-inception,
strength and association conditions remain incompletely implemented; Brihat
Jataka vocation references do not justify ranking someone's aptitude from one
indicator. Prasna Marga mixes question/natal contexts and needs an applicability
review. No scientific prediction claim or deterministic life-event inference
is authorized by these texts.

## Specific remaining blockers

1. **Interpretations:** independent classical recension/context review and a
   complete, reviewed multi-factor rule bank (including strengths, aspects and
   period-inception conditions) are still needed for the requested career,
   relationship and day/month/year personal forecasts. Their absence cannot be
   repaired by relabelling a generic reading as a calculated prediction.
2. **Saved profiles:** hosted two-user Supabase Auth testing, independent CSP/XSS
   review and a decided retention/deletion policy are still required. The live
   project was read only: subscriber profile count is zero and the candidate
   chart-vault table does not exist. No production migration was applied.
3. **Publication:** full public Kundali/prediction activation remains off until
   the applicable evidence above is complete. Passing synthetic arithmetic or
   simulated-JWT tests must not be recorded as independent release approval.

## Reproduction

The workflow `.github/workflows/wss08-release-foundations.yml` reproduces pinned
timezone tables, independent Varga/Daśā comparisons, input/view tests, disposable
PostgreSQL RLS and English/Hindi browser QA at 1440/390/320 pixels. It preserves
synthetic evidence and screenshots as CI artifacts. No public HTML, active
Panchang renderer, Word bank, live publication gate or live database is changed.
